## The one thing to remember

The rubric puts **75% of the weight on Zenon impact + technical depth + feasibility**. That means the winning idea is not the flashiest — it's the one that (a) demonstrably saves money/time or drives revenue, (b) is genuinely *agentic* (not a chatbot wrapper), and (c) can ship a real, reproducible prototype in ~6 build-weeks on **synthetic data**. Ideas that ride Zenon's existing domain expertise (forecasting, credit risk, data quality) win on all three at once.

## Open inputs that would sharpen this

These aren't blockers, but answering them lets us pick with confidence:
1. **Track preference** — do we want a client-facing flagship (Track A), a lower-risk internal tool (Track B), or an original open-category play (Track C)?
2. **Team size & skills** — 2 or 3 people? Who's strong on ML/forecasting vs. app/infra vs. front-end/demo?
3. **Appetite** — swing for the fences (highest ceiling) vs. safe execution (highest floor)?
