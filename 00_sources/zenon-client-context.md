## Zenon positioning (from LinkedIn corpus — see `zenon-linkedin-posts.md`)

Zenon's public posture: **production-grade agentic AI for PE-backed portfolio companies** (financial services, healthcare, insurance), explicitly anti-"GenAI POC theater," with a named delivery methodology ("SCORE") and a recurring "fix the data foundation first" thesis (pitched as 4–8 week engagements). Recurring agentic themes in posts: autonomous KYC/AML/credit workflows, healthcare revenue-cycle agents (denials, prior auth). Implication: **the winning idea should read like something Zenon's PE/VC practice could sell to a portfolio company — mid-market scale, months-not-years payback, production-credible governance.**

## Targeting implications

1. **Finance dominates** — 9+ of 14 logos. Ravi's priority order: finance ≫ pharma ~ media ~ retail.
2. **The freshest muscle:** collections (Barclays), HOA/SMB banking (WAB), consumer lending productionization (Lendmark), payments loyalty (ampliFI), agentic payroll workflows (ADP). Ideas adjacent to live work = instant "Zenon impact 25%" credibility and a named first customer.
3. **Crossover plays** score originality without leaving the domain: Dow Jones Risk & Compliance data × bank onboarding/screening; ampliFI loyalty × card issuers; ADP payroll rails × SMB banking.
4. Ravi is **not rigid on these specific clients** — staying in-domain (finance top, then pharma/media/retail) matters more than the exact logo.
