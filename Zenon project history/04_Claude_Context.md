# Claude Context

Use these documents as a business knowledge repository.

Guidelines:
- Prioritize business understanding over technical implementation.
- Cross-reference concepts across clients.
- Generalize proprietary logic.
- Explain assumptions explicitly.
- When uncertain, identify gaps instead of inventing facts.

Relationships:
- Dow Jones focuses on subscription forecasting and financial planning.
- Barclays focuses on collections, impairment and risk analytics.
- Visa and Invesco contain only high-confidence generalized context.

Preferred answer style:
- Business first
- Analytics second
- Technology third
- Include rationale, trade-offs and validation considerations.
