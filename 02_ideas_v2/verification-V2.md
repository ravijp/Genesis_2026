### D9 — Life-event administration

**C33 · Wind-Down Clerk (G4-1).** Claims: final-payroll/941/W-2 filings on dissolution, merchant-account
early-termination fees, multi-month closure timelines. Verdict: VERIFIED-KNOWLEDGE — Form 941 (quarterly
federal tax return) and W-2 are real, correctly named; LLC dissolution requiring state filing + final tax
account closures + license cancellation is standard practice across all US states. Merchant-account

QuickBooks Live and bookkeeping services handle final-payroll mechanics) but none owns the *whole*
cross-institution task graph with bank-transaction-based obligation discovery + a completion certificate.
No incumbent does closure-as-a-service end to end. Net: credible white-space wedge for a bank that already
holds the transaction ledger.

**C34 · Keys to the Shop (G4-2).** Claims: banks freeze accounts on sole-signer death, LOC personal
guarantees stranding families, surety-notification deadlines. Verdict: VERIFIED-KNOWLEDGE — banks freezing
a deceased sole owner's accounts pending estate administration is standard, well-documented practice

sole-proprietor SMB owner at mass-market price with an always-on peacetime+wartime agent; closest adjacent
is basic "beneficiary/POD" account features, which don't cover payroll continuity or vendor notification.
Tag **F1** — genuine white space for the SMB segment specifically.

**C35 · Bank Transplant (G4-3).** Claim: SMB bank-switching rates are very low. Verdict: VERIFIED-KNOWLEDGE
