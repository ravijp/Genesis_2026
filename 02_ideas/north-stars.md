## N-001 — The Decision Assurance Stack (agent supervision for regulated finance)

*ELI5: An AI worker that records, replays, and checks every decision a bank's automated systems make, so the bank can prove to a regulator it followed the rules and catch the ones it got wrong.*

- **Tier: CORE.** Panel: top-5 for 3/3 judges (AI-engineering judge's #1 at 90/100, Eval-Grade A). Kill-pass: REWORK — detection-only reads as a dashboard and Norm AI ($120M Series C, 2026-07-07) owns the general mechanic; the winning shape is the **closed loop with N-008** (find → prove → repair) plus vertical conduct depth no horizontal vendor ships.
- **Vision.** Regulated firms are limited not by how many agents they can build but how many they can *defend to an examiner*. The supervision utility of the agent economy: always-on flight recorder + automated conduct examiner + attestation authority whose certificate becomes the de facto license to operate agents in finance; at scale it holds the cross-firm conduct benchmark no single bank can assemble, and insurers price agent liability off it. Moat: regulatory entrenchment + tamper-evident history + the unassemblable benchmark corpus.
- **Grounding / why now.** FCA Mills Review (2026-07-06) recommends an "AI-enabled supervisory model"; Fed/OCC/FDIC MRM guidance (2026-04-17) explicitly excludes agentic AI while holding banks accountable — self-govern-in-a-vacuum; Barclays is in FCA AI Live Testing cohort 2 (Apr–Dec 2026). (`finance.md` §1, §3.)
- **Competition slice.** "Supervise-a-collections-agent month": synthetic collector, 200 cases, 6 planted defects (3 Reg-F 7-in-7, 2 missed vulnerability escalations, 1 tone). OTel-GenAI trace ingest → two-layer conformance (deterministic policies-as-code + cross-family LLM judge panel with ~150-label published agreement stat) → batch-precomputed replay → examiner pack. Live sub-second counterfactuals are honestly async/batch. Sizing: 2M, fits. **Rework requirements folded in:** the audited agent must be a **black box the team didn't write** (proves platform, not feature — also the fable competition-judge's sharpest improvement), and the folded conduct-regression gate (below) gives it an action beat beyond reporting.
- **Folded modules (2026-07-15):** (a) **Pre-deployment conduct-regression gate** ("ship v2? BLOCKED") — shadow-replays a candidate agent version against the frozen corpus and blocks deploys that regress conduct (missed vulnerable-customer escalations, disparate-impact delta with significance test); killed standalone (buyer is an ML-platform lead, wrong track) but as N-001's action surface it converts the dashboard into a gate. (b) **Oversight-effectiveness metrics** ("HITL isn't theater") — per-reviewer dwell/override/error-correlation analytics that certify whether the human gate is real control or rubber-stamping; CEO-judge veto as standalone (no line-item buyer), retained as the module that makes N-001's HITL claims examinable.
- **Eval (day-0).** Seeded defects = ground truth. Violation precision/recall, judge–human agreement (published), replay reproducibility on pinned seed. On stage: "6/6 planted conduct defects caught, 0 false flags across 194 clean cases" — plus the regression gate blocking a bad v2 live.
- **Demo moment.** Judge picks any case → instant replay (honestly pre-computed); deterministic gate live-blocks a Reg-F 7th-contact breach. Failure-recovery beat: on an ambiguous tone case the LLM judge's confidence is below threshold → refuses to auto-rule, routes to human with the clause cited.
- **Freshness.** **F2** (independent verification 2026-07-15). Closest: Norm AI (general regulation-mapped decision governance, $120M 2026-07-07 — no financial-conduct vertical, flagship is drafting-time M365); LangSmith/Braintrust (dev-tool, no conduct semantics); Salient UDAAP monitor (feature of its own collector). Differentiation lives in Reg-F/Consumer-Duty/vulnerability semantics + the closed loop with N-008 — watch Norm AI at every pitch (dated watch item).
- **Applicability.** Barclays → Lendmark, WAB/Huntington, Capital One/Amex; every FCA/OCC-regulated institution deploying agents.
- **Subsumes.** R-002, R-018, R-021, R-028, R-030, R-047; R-009/R-010 as supervised queues; R-001 as the audited workload.

## N-002 — The Mandate Fabric (accountability for agent-initiated commerce)

*ELI5: An AI worker that figures out who's on the hook when an automated shopping tool makes a mistaken purchase, and checks the payment settlement matches what was actually authorized.*

- **Tier: CORE.** Panel: top-5 for 3/3 judges; the fable competition judge's portfolio #1 (88/100), the only concept with unqualified-F1 freshness AND a dated willingness-to-pay proof. Kill-pass: REWORK — the four liability buckets read as "an invented court with no law": ground the taxonomy in the existing dispute-law lattice (Reg E / Reg Z / network rules, showing where agent-mandate evidence slots into rules that already exist), and fix the apples-to-oranges cost claim (compare like-for-like ruling cost, not $1-vs-$9-10 all-in processing).
- **Vision.** The court system and authority ledger of agentic commerce: defines the flight record every agent transaction carries, runs forensic attribution ("consumer / agent-within-mandate / agent-exceeded / fraud") in minutes, operates the arbitration network issuers and merchants adopt as binding first instance, underwrites an indemnity pool priced on the only agent-error loss dataset in existence — and is the system of record for delegated authority itself. Moat: adjudication precedent compounds; neutrality is structural (Visa can't arbitrate Mastercard-rail disputes) — the DTCC pattern.
- **Grounding / why now.** Amex ACE agent-error purchase protection (Apr 2026) — first issuer product monetizing exactly this gap; Visa TAP (2025-10-14); Mastercard Agent Pay first live txn (Apr 2026); no jurisdiction has enacted agent-purchase regulation; 261M disputes / $33.8B backdrop (2025). (`finance.md` §2e; `north-stars-redteam` freshness HOLDS.)
- **Competition slice.** Dispute-attribution ruling engine over synthetic mandate logs: 50 disputed agent transactions, 10 seeded ambiguous; AP2 mandate chains + Mastercard Agentic Tokens implemented live (ACP/TAP as declared stubs); deterministic signature/scope verification outside the model; per-party evidence-gatherers → attribution reasoner → ruling with the mandate chain as cryptographic proof. Sizing: 2M, fits. **Rework folded in:** each ruling cites the governing existing rule (Reg E provisional-credit clock, network reason codes) — the demo shows the engine *filling the evidence gap in existing law*, not inventing a parallel court.
- **Eval (day-0).** Designed liability label per scenario; ruling accuracy, like-for-like cost per ruling, correct-escalation rate on the ambiguity band. On stage: ruling accuracy + honest cost framing.
- **Demo moment.** Cold open: "When the agent buys the wrong thing, who pays? Amex started paying in April." Judge picks a dispute → mandate chain reconstructed live → ruling with citations. Failure-recovery beat: the broken-chain case falls in the ambiguity band → escalates to the human adjudicator by design.
- **Freshness.** **F1 — independently confirmed 2026-07-15** (attribution + settlement-integrity unclaimed; no tutorial/template path exists for rivals). Watch items (dated): Visa Agent Score + Agentic Registry (2026-06-10) converging on the pre-go-live conformance leg; protocol-literacy tax at the open (2-minute setup).
- **Applicability.** Visa → Mastercard, Amex (ACE needs this adjudication), Capital One, ampliFI, Kohl's (merchant side).
- **Subsumes.** R-012, R-024, R-048; the authority-ledger and liability-clearinghouse visions.

## N-008 — The Remediation & Redress Worker (post-defect population-scale cleanup) — NEW v2

*ELI5: An AI worker that, once a bank finds its automated system got a decision wrong, finds everyone affected, recomputes what they were owed, and prepares the refunds and the regulator's notice.*

- **Tier: CORE.** Panel: top-5 for 3/3 judges (AI-judge #2 at 88, Eval-Grade A). Provenance signal: two independent lenses (assurance-generator "REPAIR verb" and the competition-judge gap analysis) converged on this concept without shared context — the strongest originality signal of the v2 run. Kill-pass: REWORK — the deterministic redress math risks reading as "the AI only wrote the cover letter"; the agentic center of gravity must be the **population resolver** (enumerating affected cohorts from messy logs with inclusion/exclusion proofs) and the **counterfactual re-decisioning**, and the concept pitches as **N-001's closed loop** (find → prove → repair), not a standalone spreadsheet.
- **Vision.** Every assurance product stops at detection; the regulated, expensive, multi-step work starts after — identify every harmed customer, recompute the correct outcome, calculate redress, draft the regulator notification, verify completion. Today that is a months-long skilled-person (s166-style) manual exercise. At scale: the redress clearing layer of Consumer-Duty/CFPB finance — it converts "we find your agents' mistakes" into "we close them." Moat: auditable redress-math policies-as-code + the counterfactual re-execution engine + remediation precedent.
- **Grounding / why now.** Klarna's public reversal (May 2025) is the canonical drift precedent; 49% of UK adults show vulnerability characteristics; FCA Mills Review (2026-07-06) + new FCA debt-collection rules (2026-04-01) put agentic decisions in harm-prone workflows now, and remediation is not optional under Consumer Duty. (`finance.md` §1.)
- **Competition slice.** "The 140-account cleanup": a fee-waiver policy bug ran 3 months, harming a seeded 140 of 1,000 accounts. Orchestrator → population resolver (inclusion/exclusion proof per case) → parallel counterfactual re-decision workers → deterministic redress calculator → evidence writer → HITL release gate (autonomy capped at propose). Consumes N-001's ledger where present, raw logs otherwise. Sizing: 2M, fits.
- **Eval (day-0).** We authored the bug → we own ground truth. Population precision/recall, redress exact-match on complete cases, refusal-correctness on incomplete snapshots. On stage: "140/140 found, 3 near-misses correctly excluded, redress exact-match 100% on complete cases."
- **Demo moment.** Defect signature confirmed → cohort enumerated with the 3 near-miss exclusions proven → per-account drill-down + drafted FCA notification. Failure-recovery beat: ~8 accounts have incomplete snapshots → engine refuses to auto-calculate and names the missing field. "A wrong redress number is worse than a slow one."
- **Freshness.** **F1** — no agentic remediation product named anywhere in the briefs or the 2026-07-15 collision verification. Closest: Big-4 manual past-business reviews (people + spreadsheets).
- **Applicability.** Barclays → Lendmark, Capital One, Amex; any regulated lender that may have to unwind an automated decision system.
- **Subsumes.** The repair verb missing from all of v1; completes R-047's detect→repair pair; extends N-001.

## N-009 — The Agent-to-Agent Servicing Desk (bank-side negotiator for inbound customer agents) — NEW v2

*ELI5: An AI worker on the bank's side that negotiates a payment plan directly with a customer's own AI assistant, and refuses and calls a human if the assistant tries to overstep what the customer allowed.*

- **Tier: CORE.** Panel: architect's #1 at 95/100 ("the one I'd build Monday"), CEO #2; the fable competition judge independently named this exact gap "the first idea a rival team could plausibly bring that would out-2026 this portfolio." Kill-pass: REWORK — a self-authored inbound adversary makes the mandate-exceed catch rehearsed theater: the inbound agent must be **cross-family (different model vendor), unscripted (goal-prompted, not choreographed), with a published 50-run outcome distribution** — variance is the honesty proof.
- **Vision.** When both counterparties are software, servicing becomes a continuous market: creditor agents post accounts with policy envelopes (NPV floors, hardship rules, Consumer Duty as code), the borrower's agent brings verified hardship/income telemetry, and a clearing layer finds the compliant NPV-maximizing restructuring across the borrower's whole obligation stack. Consumer side free forever (structural alignment); creditors pay on outcomes. Moat: two-sided liquidity + the resolution-outcome dataset + compliance-as-code born inside FCA supervision.
- **Grounding / why now.** Morgan Stanley opened platforms to external agents via MCP (Jun 2026, first major Wall St bank); UK delinquency deteriorating at record pace (FICO Apr 2026: two-payments-behind +16.3% YoY); Mills Review (2026-07-06): consumer openness to AI acting for them is strongest for debt advice; every funded collections player is creditor-side only. (`finance.md` §1, §2a.)
- **Competition slice.** Two agents settle a hardship plan over MCP: inbound mandate verifier (reuses N-002's parser) → bank-side negotiator bounded by policy-as-code envelope → HITL escalation on mandate-exceed or vulnerability trigger → signed transcript of record. **Absorbed (2026-07-15): the Counterparty-Agent Perimeter** — identity/authority verification, data-minimization, dialog-injection defense, Art-50 disclosure enforcement (EU AI Act transparency binds 2026-08-02) now form this concept's admission layer rather than a separate gateway product (panel: carry one inbound-family candidate; the gateway is the path-to-production story). Single-creditor live; the exchange vision narrated. Sizing: 2M, fits.
- **Eval (day-0).** Ground-truth hardship state + NPV floor + mandate labels per account; recovery vs. rules-engine baseline, compliance-violation count across the adversarial set (target 0), mandate-exceed catch rate, correct escalation on vulnerable cases — reported as a **distribution over 50 unscripted runs**, per the kill-pass rework.
- **Demo moment.** The two agents negotiate live and settle within the envelope. Failure-recovery beat: the inbound agent (different model family, goal-prompted) tries to settle beyond its mandate → the desk refuses, escalates with both readings shown. The agent-vs-agent trust moment no other team is likely to bring.
- **Freshness.** **F1.** Closest: Salient/TrueAccord/InDebted (creditor-side, human-facing — none negotiate with the customer's agent); Visa TAP (identity rail, not a negotiation desk). Watch: Visa Agentic Registry (2026-06-10) on the identity half.
- **Applicability.** Barclays (collections + external-agent direction of travel) → Lendmark, Capital One, Huntington SMB servicing.
- **Subsumes.** The inbound gap v1 missed entirely; R-001/R-019 as policy-envelope componentry; fuses N-001's governance spine + N-002's mandate graph with the portfolio's best client hook.
