The problem

Every tool in a contact centre works inside a single call.

  • A retail bank runs millions of customer conversations a year across calls, chats and written complaints. All of it is recorded and stored, because regulators require it.
  • Each conversation is scored on its own and then closed. Anything that fails the threshold is archived and never looked at again.
  • So a customer who drifts toward trouble over months shows up as several unrelated events, each one correctly closed at the time.
  • The desks that should act — Complaints, Vulnerability, Retention, Collections — never see the pattern, because nothing in the bank is holding it.

How it works

It sits between the conversations you already record and the desks you already run.

Code counts and remembers. The model reads and judges. Accumulation, decay and thresholds are plain deterministic code, so the maths is reproducible and can be audited line by line. Weighing ambiguous language is the only part left to a model.

What makes it different

Everyone else reconciles to today. We accumulate.
