**Read:** Customer-facing chat agents (Ask Macy's, Sparky, Magic Apron, Mylow) are the most mature and best-quantified (AOV, revenue/visit lifts all publicly cited). Internal/back-office agent deployments (store-ops copilots, supply-chain exception agents) are earlier-stage, mostly framed as platform templates (Microsoft) rather than named retailer case studies with hard numbers — a gap a 6-week demo can credibly fill.

## 3. Kohl's specifically — business situation 2025-2026

- **Leadership:** Michael Bender confirmed as permanent CEO (appointed Nov 2025); has launched a "back-to-basics" revamp centered on pricing, inventory management, and brand positioning ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/kohl-ceo-tells-customers-major-033300054.html); [Capital Digest](https://capitaldigest.com/kohls-ceo-outlines-2026-strategy-during-earnings-call/)).
- **Financials:** FY2026 guidance held at flat-to-down 2% net/comp sales, adjusted EPS $1.00-$1.60. Q1 FY2026 (quarter ended May 2): net sales $3.0B, down 1.7% YoY; comp sales down 1.1% YoY ([Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/earnings/kohls-holds-annual-targets-steady-as-ceo-benders-turnaround-gains-traction-4714624)). Prior Q4 (reported ~Mar 10, 2026): sales down 3.9%, comps down 2.8%, but profit/cash flow improved ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/03/10/kohls-stock-earnings-traffic-2026-outlook); [thestreet.com](https://www.thestreet.com/retail/kohls-makes-major-2026-decision-on-store-closures)) — the classic "profitable but shrinking, traffic-starved" turnaround profile.
- **Store footprint:** Closed 27 underperforming stores across 15 states in 2025; no further 2026 closures planned; >90% of ~1,150 remaining stores now profitable. Bender: future depends less on footprint, more on **execution — pricing, inventory management, brand positioning** ([AInvest](https://www.ainvest.com/news/kohl-store-strategy-2026-major-closures-focus-shifts-profitability-2603/); [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/kohls-stores-closing-status-update-department-11693601)).
- **Sephora partnership:** Deepening — new brands (Tarte, Charlotte Tilbury) joining Sephora-at-Kohl's shelves by end of 2026, alongside MAC, YSL, Valentino, OUI; explicit strategy to draw younger shoppers while retaining loyalists ([Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/earnings/kohls-holds-annual-targets-steady-as-ceo-benders-turnaround-gains-traction-4714624)).
- **Amazon returns:** Kohl's remains the long-standing Amazon returns drop-off partner (no new 2026-specific expansion/contraction news surfaced this pass) — but this is precisely the counter-facing node most exposed to the returns-fraud/abuse pattern documented in Section 2, since Kohl's absorbs physical Amazon returns traffic and associated fraud risk without controlling the transaction.
- **Where an agentic pilot plausibly moves the needle:** Bender's own language ("pricing, inventory management, brand positioning") maps almost one-to-one onto the **markdown/pricing** and **inventory exception** nodes above. Given the CEO has publicly named execution on pricing/inventory as the lever (not store count, not traffic acquisition), a markdown-optimization or inventory-exception agent is the most on-thesis pitch for a Kohl's-branded Track A demo — it argues the numbers the CEO is already being graded on in earnings calls. The Sephora shop-in-shop is a second-order opportunity (an associate copilot spanning two brand experiences / two SKU catalogs in one footprint), and the Amazon-returns counter is a third: returns-fraud detection at a high-physical-returns-volume node.

## 4. Retail agent vendor/startup map

- **Sierra AI**: raised **$950M Series E, May 2026**, led by GV and Tiger Global, at a **$15.8B post-money valuation**; funds earmarked for "Agent OS," easier non-technical deployment, and expansion into sales/engagement workflows. Retail/consumer logos: WeightWatchers, SiriusXM, Sonos, ADT ([LeadrPro](https://www.leadrpro.com/blog/sierra-s-950m-round-makes-ai-customer-agents-a-buyer-trust-test); [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/sierra/)).
- **Decagon**: valued **$4.5B, Jan 2026** Series D ($250M, led by Coatue + Index Ventures); completed employee tender at same valuation Mar 2026. Logos skew internet-native (Duolingo, Notion, Webflow) rather than physical retail ([Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/decagon/); [Upstarts Media](https://www.upstartsmedia.com/p/decagon-sierra-ai-amazing-race)).
- **Read — commodity vs. interesting:** Sierra and Decagon are becoming the default "buy" layer for conversational customer-care agents — competing here is a commodity trap for a 6-week internal build. The **more interesting, less-saturated territory** is exactly the nodes vendors haven't fully claimed: agentic **returns-fraud detection** (Domo has a named product but the space is fragmented — Riskified, in-house builds), **loyalty-offer adjudication under agentic commerce** (Eagle Eye flags the readiness gap but no dominant agent-native winner yet), and **markdown/pricing agents** (Revionics only reaching alpha multi-agent in 2026 — still early). Microsoft's Store Operations Agent is the one node with a clear, well-resourced incumbent (Copilot Studio) — avoid head-on competition there unless the pitch is explicitly "better than Copilot for X."

## Implications for Genesis Track A

1. Customer-facing chat agents (Ask Macy's, Sparky, Magic Apron) are now table stakes with public ROI numbers — a Track A demo competing there needs a sharper wedge than "another shopping assistant."
2. Kohl's CEO has publicly and repeatedly named **pricing and inventory execution** as the turnaround lever — a markdown/inventory-exception agent demo speaks directly to metrics Kohl's leadership is already reporting on.
3. **Returns-fraud/abuse detection** is the highest-conviction node: massive quantified pain ($100B+/yr, NRF), a live Kohl's exposure point (Amazon returns counter), an ampliFI-adjacent fraud/trust angle, and no dominant agent-native incumbent yet.
4. Loyalty/offer agents are a strong ampliFI crossover and industry-acknowledged as unsolved ("most loyalty programs aren't ready for agents") — original enough to score on originality (15 pts) while staying feasible.
5. Avoid catalog/content-ops and generic store-ops-copilot demos — both are commoditized (content) or already owned by a well-funded incumbent (Microsoft Copilot Studio for store ops).
6. Internal/back-office agents (vs. customer-facing) are the underexploited demo space industry-wide — fewer named case studies exist, meaning a credible synthetic-data build reads as more novel to judges than yet another shopping chatbot.

## Candidate use-case seeds

1. **Returns-fraud triage agent** — flags AI-faked damage claims/return-abuse patterns in real time • agentic because it must reason over claim text+image+history, not just rules • demo moment: agent catches a synthetic "damaged item" photo submission and auto-routes to human review • Kohl's hook: Amazon returns counter exposure + ampliFI fraud-trust crossover.
2. **Markdown/pricing exception copilot** — recommends markdown timing/depth per SKU-store against margin targets • agentic because it must weigh competing signals (sell-through, competitor price, season) and justify a recommendation • demo moment: live before/after margin simulation on synthetic SKU data • Kohl's hook: directly answers CEO Bender's "pricing execution" turnaround language.
3. **Inventory allocation exception agent** — detects and resolves stockout/overstock mismatches across stores • agentic because root-cause + re-allocation recommendation requires multi-step reasoning • demo moment: agent reallocates synthetic inventory from an overstocked store to a stockout store and shows the working-capital delta • Kohl's hook: same "inventory management" turnaround plank.
4. **Loyalty-offer adjudication agent** — decides sub-second personalized offers at checkout in an agent-mediated (ACP/AP2) world • agentic because it must reconcile brand economics + member tier + real-time context faster than legacy batch segmentation • demo moment: two synthetic shoppers (one loyalty member, one agent-mediated anonymous buyer) get differently adjudicated offers in real time • ampliFI hook: direct product-line extension for ampliFI's card-linked-offers business.
5. **Sephora-at-Kohl's associate copilot** — cross-catalog associate assistant spanning two brand experiences in one footprint • agentic because it must route between two distinct product/policy knowledge bases • demo moment: associate asks a hybrid Sephora/Kohl's question and gets a correctly-sourced answer • Kohl's hook: literal current strategic bet (Sephora partnership expansion through 2026).
6. **Markdown-to-Sephora-shelf reallocation agent** — extension of #2/#5: reassigns physical shelf space from underperforming Kohl's apparel SKUs to expanding Sephora footprint • agentic because it's a constrained-optimization + negotiation problem across two P&Ls • demo moment: synthetic store-plan reshuffle with projected revenue-per-sq-ft delta • Kohl's hook: ties directly to the Sephora expansion narrative.
7. **Card-linked-offer fraud/abuse agent** — detects synthetic/gamed offer redemption patterns • agentic because it must correlate loyalty, card, and return-fraud signals together • demo moment: agent flags a ring of accounts gaming a promo • ampliFI hook: sits squarely in ampliFI's card-processing/loyalty core business, a strong Zenon dual-client crossover.
8. **Vendor/supplier exception-management agent** — automates supply disruption detection + re-routing recommendations • agentic because it chains detection → root cause → recommended action → human approval • demo moment: synthetic supplier delay triggers agent-proposed reallocation across DCs • Kohl's hook: general applicability to "inventory management" plank, lower originality than #1-4.
9. **Store associate task-prioritization copilot** — competes with Microsoft's Store Operations Agent but scoped narrower (e.g., markdown-execution tasks only) • agentic because it must prioritize across competing floor tasks in real time • demo moment: agent reprioritizes associate task queue when a markdown event hits • Kohl's hook: execution-focused, but risks looking like a Copilot Studio clone — use only with a sharp differentiator.
10. **Agent-mediated commerce fraud/trust layer** — cross-cutting: verifies legitimate shopping agents vs. bot/scraper traffic at the retailer's edge (Visa Trusted Agent Protocol-adjacent) • agentic because it must classify traffic intent in real time • demo moment: agent distinguishes a legitimate ChatGPT/Gemini shopping agent from a scraping bot and applies differential access • Kohl's/ampliFI hook: connects back to the protocol-fragmentation white space already flagged in `retail-agentic-commerce.md` — good if Track A wants to bridge both research files.
