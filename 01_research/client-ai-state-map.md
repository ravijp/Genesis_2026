## Reading for ideation (the frame, not conclusions)

1. **The modal client is S1-S2, not S0:** they have GenAI running (productivity tools, summarization, servicing assist) and stated agentic intent, but **no agentic workflow automation in production**. The sellable move is the *first governed production agent on a workflow they already feel* — or a governance/QC layer over AI decisions they already make.
2. **The 3-week-fit discriminator:** surfaces that already exist as files/exports/transcripts (Barclays transcripts, WAB lockbox files, ampliFI redemption logs, HNB telemetry) fit in weeks; anything needing core-banking write access, real-time streams, or new rails does not.
3. **Regulated-pilot posture is an asset:** Barclays (FCA sandbox) and Lendmark (productionization roadmap) are *already trying* to do this safely — a build that arrives with evals, HITL gates, and an examiner-ready audit trail plugs into programs that exist, which is the difference between "exciting demo" and "1-year engagement."
4. **Don't out-build S3 clients on their own turf** (CapOne conversational, MS advisor tools, Visa rails); sell them assurance, conformance, or a queue they haven't automated.
